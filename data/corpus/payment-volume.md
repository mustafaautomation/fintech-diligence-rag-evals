# Payment volume

## Volume and take rate

Total payment volume for the year ended December 31, 2025 was $18,750,000,000, and the reported take rate was 65.0 basis points.
Total payment volume for the year ended December 31, 2024 was $16,400,000,000, and the reported take rate was 60.0 basis points.
The take rate is payment processing revenue divided by total payment volume.
