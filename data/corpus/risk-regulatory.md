# Regulatory risk

## Licenses

At December 31, 2025, Harborline Payments, Inc. held money-transmitter licenses in 48 states and the District of Columbia, and it did not hold a license in Montana or Nevada.
The company also holds a United Kingdom e-money permission.
No enforcement action was recorded during the year ended December 31, 2025.
A money-transmitter examination by the State of Texas closed in September 2025 with no findings.

## License risk

Loss of a money-transmitter license would restrict payment activity in that state. This risk factor does not add a license count beyond the licenses section.
