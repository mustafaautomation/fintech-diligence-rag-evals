# Customers

## Largest customer

Northwind Retail Group was the largest customer and accounted for $20,876,800 of revenue, equal to 11.2% of total revenue, for the year ended December 31, 2025.

## Customer concentration

The ten largest customers accounted for 38.5% of total revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025, equal to $71,764,000.

## Active customers

Active business customers at December 31, 2025 numbered 1,240, compared with 980 at December 31, 2024.
